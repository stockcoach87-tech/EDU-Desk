BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Upload the contents of this folder to your hosting provider's public_html (or equivalent web root).
2. In script.js, replace YOUR-EMAIL@example.com with a real monitored email address.
3. Review the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use so they accurately describe your real operations and comply with applicable law.
4. If collecting contact messages, connect a real form backend or verified email service. The current form prepares an email on the visitor's device; it does not store or send submissions itself.
5. Configure HTTPS/SSL for https://edu-dash.shop and test all links on desktop and mobile.
6. Use only accurate business details and services. A website alone does not guarantee Meta or WhatsApp approval.

This template intentionally avoids making claims about specific products or services.
